1.20.1-0.7.5-beta.1 (beta)

Five beta patches (one per feat) since 0.7.0: Halloween matrix port, Distiller 2×2, Cooler GUI, chiseled/standing discs, jar/vessel wiring + vanilla flower icon. Also ice cream / dulce de leche. Matrix: **1.20.1 / 1.21.1 / 26.1 / 26.2**. Includes prior 0.7.0-beta.2 notes — see below.

Distiller (3D layout)

- New alembic model and textures (copper / iron / wood / glass); lit vs unlit heater look
- Footprint as a 2×2 multiblock (`distiller_satellite`) so the condenser table does not collide with players
- Output bottles render in-world via Placeable Decor BER (not baked into the block model)
- GUI atlas refresh: gray slot placeholders; dedicated salt-result slot (`SIZE=7`)
- Condenser water shows a light frost overlay when coolant (snowball / ice / snow / packed / blue ice) is in the snow slot
- Coolant already consumes 1 unit per finished batch; per-type duration balance still TBD

Cooler GUI

- `cooler.png` layout polish for all six modes (GRID / COMBO labels)
- Ghost slot tint gray; horizontal double open/close uses freezer sound
- Front/lid z-fight and door light sealing fixes (playtest still open)

Halloween candy basket

- Connected U-handle on item and placed models: original body sprite + opaque handle cutout; thinner crossbar; candy-colored mounts

Ice cream / dulce de leche

- Sweetened milk: milk bucket + sugar (bucket returns). Pours into a bottle, a jar, and a placed bottle
- Colored sugar: sugar + one dye, one item per dye. Dye + milk has no recipe
- Flavor: milk + sugar + dye, sweetened milk + dye, or milk + colored sugar. Snow cauldron stores that color; the cone scoops it
- Dulce de leche: empty cauldron over fire, a lit campfire, lava, or magma, with milk + two sugar + vanilla powder (sweetened milk counts as one sugar)
- Vanilla flower on dirt (not sand); harvest the pod and craft it into powder
- Vanilla flower item icon remastered: shorter stem, cream bloom with pixel shading (oxeye/cornflower style)
- Flavor icons are 16×16 placeholders
- Multi-scoop ice cream (up to 3 balls, 6 servings per full snow cauldron, food names), ice cream carton, jar sprinkles from colored sugar, Cones & Sprinkles disc, freezer hum and open-door fog, tameable fox, sit on stairs. Code on 1.20.1, 1.21.1, 26.1, and 26.2. Pastel snow tint is 1.20.1 and 1.21.1 only. On 1.20.1 the disc is a record item, not a jukebox song

1.20.1-0.6.0-alpha.1 (alpha)

Water landscape dye + license/links polish. Store alpha for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge, Fabric, NeoForge).
Ports on the same stores: 1.21.1 (Forge / Fabric / NeoForge) and 26.1 / 26.2 (Fabric + NeoForge).

- Water Dye Diffuser: craft (iron + glass + bucket); fill with water (bucket, bottle, cauldron, or ocean/river); one dye color only (pink/lime/green/cyan/purple/magenta/red) up to 64 charges
- Load dye on the placed block, with both hands, or by right-clicking dye onto the item in inventory; at 64 the canister seals (no more dye / no sneak-empty)
- Paint: right-click water while holding a filled diffuser, or empty-hand use on a placed ready diffuser; sneak+empty removes a nearby stain
- Persistent circular color overlay on lakes/oceans (vanilla water unchanged); radius scales linearly 2→16 with charges (1→64); vertical reach ±8; max 256 stains per dimension
- Breakable by hand (~1 s); config toggle `water_landscape_dye`
- License screen: QoL Author License, GitHub link; mod list homepage points to GitHub; alpha on Modrinth and CurseForge
