1.20.1-0.6.0-alpha.1 (alpha)

Water landscape dye + license/links polish. Store alpha for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge, Fabric, NeoForge).
Unreleased in-tree ports now compile: 1.21.1 (Forge / Fabric / NeoForge) and 26.1 / 26.2 (Fabric + NeoForge). Not on Modrinth/CurseForge yet.

- Water Dye Diffuser: craft (iron + glass + bucket); fill with water (bucket, bottle, cauldron, or ocean/river); one dye color only (pink/lime/green/cyan/purple/magenta/red) up to 64 charges
- Load dye on the placed block, with both hands, or by right-clicking dye onto the item in inventory; at 64 the canister seals (no more dye / no sneak-empty)
- Paint: right-click water while holding a filled diffuser, or empty-hand use on a placed ready diffuser; sneak+empty removes a nearby stain
- Persistent circular color overlay on lakes/oceans (vanilla water unchanged); radius scales linearly 2→16 with charges (1→64); vertical reach ±8; max 256 stains per dimension
- Breakable by hand (~1 s); config toggle `water_landscape_dye`
- License screen: QoL Author License, GitHub link, CurseForge coming soon; mod list homepage points to GitHub
